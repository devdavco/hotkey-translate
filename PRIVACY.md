# Hotkey Translate: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Hotkey Translate does not collect, store or sell personal data, and it has no analytics or tracking.

## What the extension handles

- Selected text: when you press a shortcut, the extension reads the text you selected on the current page. It sends that text to Google Translate, either to open the translation in a new tab or to play its pronunciation. This only happens when you press a shortcut. The extension does not keep a copy of the text.
- Language preferences: your source, target and audio languages are saved with chrome.storage.sync. If you are signed in to your browser, it syncs them across your devices. They never reach the developer.

## Third parties

Translation and audio are provided by Google Translate. Google's privacy policy applies to the text sent to its service: https://policies.google.com/privacy
